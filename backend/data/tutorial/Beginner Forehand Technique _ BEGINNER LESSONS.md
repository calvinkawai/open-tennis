## 1. Video Summary

**Technical Detail:** 
*   **Grip:** Use an Eastern forehand grip, achieved by sliding your palm down the strings to the bottom of the handle, functioning like you are "shaking hands with the Racket". Keep the grip relaxed.
*   **Ready Position:** Maintain an athletic stance with feet wider than shoulders, knees bent, weight on the balls of the feet, and the racket tilted at 45 degrees with two hands.
*   **Preparation:** Use a "unit turn" where pivoting your body to the side acts as a compact, small-to-big backswing.
*   **Contact Point:** Position the racket at the side of your waist and just in front of your body, keeping the racket straight up and down with strings facing the target.
*   **Swing Path:** Drive the racket on a low to high path to lift the ball over the net, pushing from the hip and rotating the body for power rather than just swinging the arm. 
*   **Follow-Through:** Continue the swing completely through contact until the racket rests over the opposite shoulder, with the elbow pointing down the court and the back of the hand facing the opposite cheek.

## 2. Segments

**The Grip and Ready Position**
Establish a relaxed Eastern forehand grip and an athletic stance to prepare for movement. Keep your palm facing forward in alignment with your strings to easily control the racket face. 
*   **Key coaching cue:** "it's as if you're playing tennis with your hand"
*   **Visual focus:** Watch for bent knees, feet wider than shoulders, and the racket held with two hands at a 45-degree tilt.

**The Unit Turn and Setup**
React to the approaching ball by rotating your body sideways to set up the shot. This body rotation replaces a complex arm movement and naturally sets the racket in the correct position for your forward swing.
*   **Key coaching cue:** "the turn of my body is going to be my back swing"
*   **Visual focus:** Watch the torso turn sideways, keeping the racket swing compact and starting near the waist.

**The Contact Point**
Begin your forward swing from a stable, open stance to strike the ball beside and slightly in front of your waist. Keep the racket face flat and strings directed exactly where you want the ball to go.
*   **Key coaching cue:** "joining these dots from our waist to our shoulder"
*   **Visual focus:** Watch the racket head remain straight up and down at waist level with strings pointing directly at the target.

**The Swing Path and Finish**
Execute a low to high swing, pulling the racket up and through the ball using body rotation. Do not stop the racket at impact; follow through entirely until the racket rests over the shoulder to ensure proper ball lift and direction.
*   **Key coaching cue:** "not stopping once we hit the ball without a follow through"
*   **Visual focus:** Watch the elbow pointing down the court and the back of the hand resting against the opposite cheek on the follow-through.

## 3. Cartoon Card (4 Steps)

**Step 1: The Setup**
*   **Instruction:** Stand in an athletic stance with a relaxed Eastern forehand grip.
*   **Visual:** Knees bent, feet wide, racket tilted 45 degrees. ✗ Standing with stiff, straight legs.

**Step 2: The Unit Turn**
*   **Instruction:** Turn your body sideways to the ball to naturally create your backswing.
*   **Visual:** Torso rotated sideways, racket set at waist level. ✗ Taking a massive, arm-only backswing.

**Step 3: The Contact**
*   **Instruction:** Strike the ball at waist level, slightly in front, strings facing the target.
*   **Visual:** Open stance, racket vertical at the waist, forward motion arrow. ✗ Twisting the racket face open.

**Step 4: The Finish**
*   **Instruction:** Swing low to high, finishing with the racket over your opposite shoulder.
*   **Visual:** Racket over shoulder, elbow pointing down court, upward motion arrow. ✗ Stopping the racket directly at contact.
