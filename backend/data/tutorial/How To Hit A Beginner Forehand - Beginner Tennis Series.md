## 1. Video Summary

**Technical Detail:**
*   Develop stroke mechanics by imagining the racket face is an extension of your own hand.
*   Start close to the net at the service line and progressively move back to the baseline to gradually build the full swing.
*   Drive the ball over the net using a strict low-to-high swing path, rather than just hitting with more pace.
*   Maintain a straight wrist and elbow at contact to prevent the ball from flying crooked.
*   Hold the high finish on every single shot to build correct muscle memory and verify your ending position.

## 2. Segments

**Forehand Grip Progression**
Progress the hitting hand from pushing directly behind the strings, to three fingers on the throat, down to the base of the handle, which naturally develops a proper eastern grip and a full baseline swing.
*   **Key coaching cue:** "back of the hand towards your left cheek".
*   **Visual focus:** Watch the hand placement slide down the racket in stages, ending with the elbow pointing straight forward on the high finish.

**Two-Handed Backhand Mechanics**
Start with the non-dominant hand flat behind the strings to feel the push and command the racket on the backhand side, then slide the left hand down next to the right hand to form the standard two-handed grip.
*   **Key coaching cue:** "back of your left hand towards your right cheek".
*   **Visual focus:** Watch the left hand apply forward pressure directly to the strings through the contact point before transitioning to the grip.

**Net Clearance & Swing Path**
Hitting the ball hard will not clear the net; the swing must travel upward while keeping the racket face neutral to create the correct trajectory.
*   **Key coaching cue:** "command the racket to go up".
*   **Visual focus:** Watch the racket head move from below the contact zone to a held position above the opposite shoulder without opening the racket face.

## 3. Cartoon Card (4 Steps)

**Step 1: Palm on Strings**
*   **Instruction:** Place your right hand directly behind the strings and push the ball upward.
*   **Visual:** Body open, right palm pressing strings at contact point, upward motion arrow, ✗ Bending the wrist.

**Step 2: Three-Finger Throat Grip**
*   **Instruction:** Pinch the racket throat with three fingers and swing from low to high.
*   **Visual:** Hand gripping upper throat, contact point in front, upward diagonal motion arrow, ✗ Finishing down low.

**Step 3: Upper Handle Grip**
*   **Instruction:** Move hand to the top of the handle, swinging straight through the ball.
*   **Visual:** Hand high on the grip, racket face completely vertical at contact, forward motion arrow, ✗ Opening racket face.

**Step 4: Full Baseline Swing**
*   **Instruction:** Hold the base of the grip, swing fully, and freeze your high finish.
*   **Visual:** Hand at handle base, full swing path motion arrow, frozen high finish pose, ✗ Recoiling racket instantly.
