## 1. Video Summary

**Technical Detail:** 
*   **Grip:** Use a semi-western grip to achieve an optimal balance of topspin and power through the ball.
*   **Stance/Ready:** Wait with your forehand grip established, non-hitting hand on the racket throat, and the racket head held higher than grip level to create leverage.
*   **Swing Path:** Initiate with a shoulder turn to bring the racket halfway back, extend the non-hitting arm across the body to coil the torso, and allow the racket to naturally drop into a lag position on the hitting side.
*   **Contact:** Strike the ball well in front of the body.
*   **Follow-through:** Accelerate completely through the contact zone without slowing down, finishing naturally around the opposite shoulder, chest, or hip to maintain control and prevent injury.

## 2. Segments

- **Grip & Ready Position**
- Establish a semi-western grip and hold the racket head higher than the grip with your non-hitting hand resting on the throat to build early leverage.
- Key coaching cue: "leverage is force over the ball"
- Visual focus: Watch the racket head remain visibly elevated above the hands during the initial stance.

- **The Unit Turn**
- Turn your non-hitting shoulder toward the incoming ball to automatically prepare the racket halfway back, ensuring the hitting arm remains totally relaxed.
- Key coaching cue: "turn your left shoulder"
- Visual focus: Watch the non-hitting hand stay anchored on the racket throat as the torso rotates.

- **The Power Position**
- Stretch the non-hitting arm across the baseline to coil the core obliques, ensuring there is ample space between the hitting elbow and the torso.
- Key coaching cue: keep "a lot of space between my racquets and my body"
- Visual focus: Watch the wide physical gap between the hitting arm's elbow and the body's side.

- **Natural Racket Lag**
- Uncoil your body and let the elevated racket head naturally drop behind the grip, pointing the butt cap directly at the oncoming ball. 
- Key coaching cue: "you want it to happen naturally"
- Visual focus: Watch the bottom of the racket handle point directly toward the incoming ball before the forward swing.

- **Contact & Follow-Through**
- Strike the ball out in front of your body and accelerate cleanly through the contact zone to a full finish around the body.
- Key coaching cue: "accelerating through the contact point"
- Visual focus: Watch the racket carry continuous speed past contact and wrap fully around the torso.

## 3. Cartoon Card (4 Steps)

- **Step 1: The Unit Turn**
- Turn shoulder, keep non-hitting hand on throat, and hold racket head above grip.
- Visual: Torso rotated sideways, racket head pointing up. Motion arrow showing shoulder turn. ✗ Tense, gripping hitting arm.

- **Step 2: The Power Position**
- Coil your core by bringing your non-hitting arm fully across your body.
- Visual: Non-hitting arm extended across chest, wide gap at hitting elbow. ✗ Tucked elbow jammed against ribs.

- **Step 3: Racket Lag**
- Let the racket head fall naturally behind the grip as your body uncoils.
- Visual: Racket butt cap pointing straight ahead at ball. Motion arrow uncoiling hips. ✗ Forced, aggressively cocked wrist.

- **Step 4: Contact & Follow-Through**
- Strike out in front and accelerate fully through the ball to your finish.
- Visual: Racket hitting ball ahead of body, wrapping around opposite shoulder. ✗ Stopping swing abruptly at contact point.
