# Open Tennis language

Open Tennis connects tennis reference material with a player's own practice history. Original evidence, derived understanding, and chosen exercises remain distinct.

## Language

**Technical source**: A tutorial or teaching note with an identifiable origin. Its existence does not imply that it has been reviewed or that every interpretation is correct.

**Original journal entry**: The owner's account of a practice, including completion, context, and subjective observations. It is not an objective skill score or evidence of technical causation.

**Technical wiki**: Topic-oriented derived pages grounded in technical sources, with explicit owner review before publication.

**Personal training wiki**: Topic-oriented derived understanding of the owner's practice history, with original-record citations and a distinction between observation and inference.

**Wiki version**: A complete historical state of a derived page, retaining its sources and change summary.

**Model supplement**: An explanation or suggestion not directly supported by supplied original evidence. It must not be presented as a verified technical conclusion.

**Training card**: An exercise arrangement the owner explicitly adopts, preserving its source links and unverified supplement labels.

**Local draft**: A device-local record not yet acknowledged by the server. It is neither synchronized data nor a backup.

**Integrated agent**: The application's bounded assistant for finding and organizing evidence and proposing derived content. It is not the development-time coding assistant and cannot overwrite original records or approve technical changes.
